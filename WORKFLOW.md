### Administrative Forms
PIPELINE_ADMIN in FormController maps to: Submit → Checker Approval (role 2) → Review Approval (role 4) → Grant Approval (role 6) → Completed (role 6). That's 5 sequences.

### Finance Forms
PIPELINE_FINANCE maps to: Submit → Checker Approval (role 2) → Process Approval (role 5) → Evaluation Approval (role 4) → Grant Approval (role 6) → Completed (role 6). That's 6 sequences.

- **role 2** = Approver/Supervisor
- **role 4** = Department Head/Finance Head 
- **role 5** = Checker/Acquisition 
- **role 6** = Final Approver.
